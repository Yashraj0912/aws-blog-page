What is Amazon Web Services (AWS)?

Amazon Web Services (AWS) is the world's most widely adopted cloud platform, offering over 200 fully featured services from data centers globally. AWS provides computing power, database storage, content delivery, and other functionality to businesses and developers. Whether you're running a startup or a Fortune 500 company, AWS scales with your needs — pay only for what you use, and stop wasting money on unused infrastructure.
